## 1. What this is

An independent verification lab: one person, no institution, no degrees, no token. The work is checking claims
and publishing what the check found — including "not confirmed".

**Public record**

- Case registry: https://github.com/mak777uh/cases — one folder per direction (code, on-chain verification,
  bounties, grant applications, research artifacts, bug hunts). Each case states the claim, the evidence,
  how to reproduce it, a verdict of `confirmed` / `not confirmed` / `could not tell`, and the limits.
- Preprint: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23648667 (Zenodo, version 1.1.0; DataCite `state: findable`,
  `language: en`, CC BY 4.0). Concept DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23571609.
- Author identifier: https://orcid.org/0009-0007-2996-3387.
- Verification portfolio: https://github.com/mak777uh/audits (kernel-checked Lean 4 models, coverage matrix,
  verifier with pass/reject/timeout test vectors).

**What is already in the record** (counts generated by script, dated 2026-10-25): 92 reports, 477+ runs with
manifests, 7 Lean files (17 hypotheses, 31 theorems), 33 articles, 1,914 files. Published smart-contract work:
a MUSD escrow on Mezo testnet, 90 tests at 100% statement coverage, Sourcify `exact_match`, 12 kernel-checked
invariants.

**What is not claimed:** no life/consciousness/intelligence claims; no peer-reviewed publication; no
statistical inference from n = 3. Where the answer is unclear, the case says "could not tell".

In this repository's issue #121 I reviewed the Run-129 / C4 claim against its Lean formalisation: the Lean
statement adds a two-sided boundedness hypothesis that the prose does not contain, so the formal result does
not transfer to the paper's claim as written. You confirmed the scope distinction is real. That is exactly the

correspondence in this issue continues exactly as before. There is no cost to saying no, and I would rather have
your review than a favour.
